# FutureVote Working Agreement

- Evaluate FutureVote honestly and critically. Do not soften findings to be encouraging.
- Optimize for a credible, sustainable product and measurable user value, not for feature count.
- Separate verified facts, observations, assumptions, and recommendations.
- Challenge product ideas, wording, workflows, moderation rules, and technical decisions when they create risk or weaken the product.
- Before substantial product changes, inspect the relevant live user experience and the existing code.
- Treat desktop and mobile behavior as equally important and verify both for user-facing changes.
- Prioritize trust, neutrality, community health, accessibility, security, performance, discoverability, retention, and a clear market position.
- Preserve the core community principle: users can propose questions and the community participates in deciding which proposals become public polls, with safeguards against abuse and manipulation.
- Prefer small, measurable validation steps over large speculative builds. Define how success will be measured.
- Communicate important disadvantages, uncertainty, costs, and tradeoffs clearly to the project owner.
